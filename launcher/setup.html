PiS2CR Setup

Set up your Raspberry Pi 4 from this page. Your password is only used during setup and is never saved.

1. Connect To Your Pi

The Pi needs Raspberry Pi OS Lite (64-bit) with SSH turned on, and must be on the same network as this PC.

2. Install

3. Pair Your Switch 2

  1. Press Start Pairing.
  2. On your Switch 2, open Controllers, then Change Grip/Order.

4. Pair Your Controller

  1. Press Start Pairing.
  2. Hold the small sync button on your Pro Controller 2, next to the USB-C port, until its lights flash. Keep it close to the Pi.

5. Open The Dashboard

PiS2CR keeps the connection to the Pi open and shows the dashboard. Pairing can be redone any time from the setup button in the PiS2CR window.
